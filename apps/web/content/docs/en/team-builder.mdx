## Overview

The Team Builder is your central hub for designing and managing competitive Double Battle teams (calibrated for the Pokémon Champions format).
You can register up to 6 Pokémon per team and customize items, abilities, moves, and EV spreads (with a 32-per-stat cap and 66 total cap).

Beyond basic roster management, it includes advanced features such as **Revision History** for visual diffs and one-click restores, a dedicated **Strategy Notes** workspace for tournament planning, and deep **BOX Integration** to load trained Pokémon in seconds.
Teams created here connect seamlessly with [Battle Record](/docs/battle-record) and [Battle Analytics](/docs/battle-analytics).

## Adding and Searching Pokémon

Click on any empty slot to open the Pokémon selection dialog.

### Search Capabilities

- **Name and Substring Search**: Type any Pokémon name to filter the roster (e.g., `Garchomp`).
- **Romaji & Hiragana Support**: When Japanese search is active, you can search using standard Hiragana (`がぶりあす`) as well as direct Romaji keystrokes (e.g., `gabu`, `rizadon`, `sa-fugo-`).
- **Type Filtering**: Filter directly with type tags such as `@type:Dragon` or `@type:Steel`.
- **Automatic Query Reset**: Switching slots or opening dialogs automatically clears previous queries and token chips, preventing accidental mismatching.

### Loading from My Box (BOX Integration)

Switch to the **BOX** tab in the selection dialog to browse your saved Pokémon sets from [My Box](/docs/my-box).

- Inspect items, EVs, and move sets at a glance before selecting.
- You can also click the **Load from Box** icon directly from an empty slot card or the slot detail header to load a trained set with a single click.

## Pokémon Configuration

### Item, Ability, and Nature

Configure your Pokémon's **Held Item**, **Ability**, and **Nature** from the slot inspector.
For Pokémon capable of Mega Evolution, clicking the **Mega** button instantly switches their stats, typings, and ability to the Mega-Evolved form.

### Move Selection

Click any of the 4 move slots to open the move selection drawer.
Inspect move category (Physical, Special, Status), Base Power, Accuracy, Type, and Priority bracket before assigning.

### EV Spreads (Effort Values)

Use the sliders or numeric input fields under the **EVs** tab:

- **Champions Standard**: EVs are capped at **32 per stat** and **66 in total** (Nature boosts are specified with `+` / `-`).
- **Real-Time Validation**: Allocations exceeding the caps are prevented, and remaining EV points update in real-time.
- **Bulk Optimization**: Use the built-in bulk adjustment utility to automatically compute optimal Defensive spreads (HP vs Def/SpD) to survive specific incoming damage benchmarks.

## Strategy Notes

The **Strategy Notes** workspace allows you to document your strategic thinking across dedicated sections (Build Process, Basic Concepts, Matchup Notes) directly alongside your team.

### Typical Use Cases

- **Build Rationale**: Core archetypes, synergy explanations, and win conditions.
- **Lead Selections**: Standard lead pairs and backline plans (e.g., "Lead: Flutter Mane + Chi-Yu / Back: Urshifu + Chien-Pao").
- **Matchup Guides**: Specific gameplans against common meta threats (Trick Room, Rain offense, Dondozo cores, etc.).

Strategy notes are version-controlled alongside your Pokémon compositions.

## Revision History & Restore

To ensure you can experiment freely without fear of losing previous iterations, the Team Builder tracks a complete **Revision History** for each team.

### Key Features

- **Automatic Versioning**: Every major adjustment (swapping members, reallocating EVs, tweaking moves) creates a distinct snapshot.
- **Visual Differences**:
  - **Semantic Diff**: Highlights changes to Pokémon, items, EVs, and moves in an intuitive side-by-side view (desktop).
  - **Pokepaste Text Diff**: Shows line-by-line textual diffs in Showdown format.
- **Mobile-Optimized**: A dedicated clean layout for reviewing historical versions on smartphone displays.
- **One-Click Restore**: Click "Restore this version" on any historical snapshot to revert your entire team (including strategy notes) back to that exact state.

## Import and Export

| Action                        | Description                                                                |
| :---------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Import from Pokepaste URL** | Paste a Pokepaste link to load and parse a team instantly.                 |
| **Import from Text**          | Paste raw Showdown / Pokepaste text to load a team instantly.              |
| **Copy to Clipboard**         | Copy the active team in standard Pokepaste format to your clipboard.       |
| **Create Pokepaste**          | Automatically publish your team to Pokepaste and receive a shareable link. |

## Saving and Sharing

- **Cloud Synchronization**: Authenticated users enjoy automatic cloud sync across all devices for teams, notes, and revision histories.
- **Guest Storage**: Unauthenticated sessions are securely stored in your local browser storage.
- **Shareable Links**: Generate a public link via the **Share Team** button with customizable privacy options (choose whether to disclose EV spreads).
