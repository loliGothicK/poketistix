## Overview

The Damage Calculator lets you simulate exact damage rolls, HP percentage swings, and KO thresholds (guaranteed vs. chance to OHKO/2HKO) between any attacking and defending Pokémon under customizable battlefield conditions.

Engineered specifically for the Pokémon Champions format, it natively supports Double Battle mechanics—including multi-target damage spread (0.75x), Helping Hand boosts, Friend Guard mitigation, and the 32-EV limit per stat (66 total cap).

---

## Instant BOX Loading (BOX Integration)

Click the **Load from Box** button located above either the Attacker or Defender column to pull any trained Pokémon set directly from [My Box](/docs/my-box).

- **All-in-One Configuration**: Effort Values, Nature, Held Item, Ability, and the 4 known moves are applied instantly in one click.
- **Frictionless Benchmarking**: Test real-world scenarios immediately without tedious manual entry (e.g., "Can my defensively trained Clefable survive Choice Specs Flutter Mane's Moonblast?", "Does my Landorus guarantee an OHKO on this target?").

---

## Basic Workflow

1. **Select Attacker**: Choose via name search (supports Hiragana/Romaji) or click **Load from Box**.
2. **Pick a Move**: Choose from the Pokémon's assigned moves or search the full moveset library.
3. **Select Defender**: Set the target Pokémon, defensive EV spread, and item (or load from Box).
4. **Inspect Calculations**: Review damage rolls, percentage ranges, and lethal KO probabilities updated in real-time.

---

## Configuration Parameters

Both Attacker and Defender sides provide full control over the following variables:

| Parameter                   | Details                                                                                    |
| :-------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Pokémon**                 | Standard search, Mega Evolution forms, and alternate typings.                              |
| **EVs (Effort Values)**     | 0 to 32 per stat, up to a total of 66 (Champions rules).                                   |
| **IVs (Individual Values)** | 0 to 31 (useful for Min-Speed Trick Room sets or 0 Atk Foul Play optimization).            |
| **Nature**                  | +10% positive and -10% negative stat modifiers.                                            |
| **Held Item**               | Damage-boosting items (Choice items, Life Orb, Type-boosting plates) and recovery berries. |
| **Ability**                 | Abilities affecting damage calculation (Scrappy, Huge Power, Multiscale, etc.).            |
| **Stat Boosts / Drops**     | -6 to +6 stage multipliers for Attack, Defense, Sp. Atk, and Sp. Def.                      |
| **Status Conditions**       | Burn (halves physical attack damage), Paralysis, Sleep, etc.                               |

## Battlefield & Support Conditions

Toggle Double Battle battleground states with a single click in the **Field** panel:

### Weather & Terrains

- **Weather**: Sun (Fire 1.5x / Water 0.5x), Rain (Water 1.5x / Fire 0.5x), Snow (Ice Def 1.5x), Sandstorm (Rock SpD 1.5x).
- **Terrains**: Grassy (Grass 1.3x / Earthquake halved), Electric (Electric 1.3x), Psychic (Psychic 1.3x), Misty (Dragon damage halved).

### Screens & Reductions

- **Aurora Veil / Reflect / Light Screen**: Calculated using accurate Double Battle multi-target reduction values.
- **Friend Guard**: Reduces damage received by allies by 25% (0.75x multiplier).

### Offense Enhancements

- **Helping Hand**: Boosts base move damage by 1.5x.
- **Battery / Power Spot**: Active ally damage amplifiers.

## Understanding the Results

- **KO Probability**: Clear practical benchmarks such as "Guaranteed OHKO (100%)", "87.5% chance to OHKO", or "Guaranteed 2HKO".
- **16 Damage Rolls**: Complete inspection of all 16 discrete RNG damage roll values.
- **Visual Health Bar**: Dynamic colored HP meter showing remaining HP ranges.
- **One-Click Text Copy**: Easily copy standardized damage calc results in Showdown format to share in team reports or discord.
