## Overview

My BOX is a storage feature where you can save and manage individual Pokémon independently of your teams.
By registering a Pokémon to your BOX, you can easily reuse the same trained Pokémon across multiple different teams.

## Saving Pokémon to the BOX

Open the details slot of any Pokémon in the Team Builder, and click the **"Save to BOX"** button.
The Pokémon's item, ability, nature, moves, and EV spreads will all be saved together.

## Loading and Cross-Tool Integration

Saved Pokémon can be loaded across the platform with a single click:

- **[Team Builder](/docs/team-builder)**:
  - Browse your saved sets under the "BOX" tab of the Pokémon selection dialog, with items, EVs, and moves visible before adding.
  - Load directly from empty slot cards or slot detail headers via the "Load from Box" action.
- **[Damage Calculator](/docs/damage-calc)**:
  - Use the "Load from Box" button above the Attacker or Defender column to benchmark survivability and damage output without manual entry.

## Search and Filter

You can use the search bar at the top of the BOX screen to filter your saved Pokémon.

- Search by partial name (e.g., `Dragonite`)
- Bilingual search is supported (English/Japanese)

## Deleting Pokémon

Click the **"Remove from BOX"** button on any Pokémon card to delete it from your storage.
This action will not affect any teams that are currently using this Pokémon.

## Login and Cloud Sync

If you are logged into your account, your BOX data is safely stored in the cloud.
If you are not logged in, the data is stored only in your browser's local storage.
