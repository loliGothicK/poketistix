## Overview

Battle Analytics (`/battle-analytics`) transforms raw match logs from [Battle Record](/docs/battle-record) into actionable strategic insights.

Rather than simple win/loss tallies, it lets you dissect your results across three dedicated analytical dimensions: which Pokémon deliver the highest win rate when brought, which teams perform best in the current meta, and which opposing threats cause the most trouble.

---

## The Three Analytics Tabs

Switch seamlessly between views via the tabs at the top of the analytics dashboard:

### 1. Pokémon Performance (Pick & Win Rates)

Aggregates metrics for each Pokémon registered in your active team:

| Metric                         | Description                                                                 | Strategic Insight                                                                                                               |
| :----------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Pick Count & Pick Rate (%)** | Percentage of matches where this Pokémon was selected.                      | Measures team cohesion. Identifies core linchpins vs. "dead weight" Pokémon that are rarely chosen.                             |
| **Win Rate When Picked (%)**   | Win percentage specifically in games where this Pokémon entered the match.  | Identifies your most impactful win conditions and spotlights members who might be liabilities despite high selection frequency. |
| **Lead vs. Backline Ratio**    | Breakdown of how often the Pokémon was deployed as a starter vs. a reserve. | Evaluates whether your leads are establishing early control and whether your sweepers successfully close out games.             |

### 2. Team Performance Breakdown

When managing multiple teams or testing new variations, compare your teams side-by-side:

- **Record & Win Percentage**: Pinpoint which archetype excels most in the current ranked season.
- **Iteration Tracking**: Correlate win rate spikes and drops with specific team iterations documented in [Team Builder](/docs/team-builder) revision histories.

### 3. Opponent Pokémon Matchups

Tracks your performance against every opposing Pokémon encountered across your matches:

- **Targeted Threat Identification**: Uncover hidden vulnerabilities (e.g., opposing threats where your win rate drops below 35% despite regular encounters).
- **Metagame Frequency**: Monitor the most prevalent opposing cores in your current rating bracket.

## Filters & Strategic Feedback Loop

### Season and Team Filtering

Use the header selectors to isolate specific **Seasons** or narrow your focus to a single **Team**.

### The Iterative Improvement Loop

1. **Analyze**: Identify problematic opposing Pokémon in the Opponent Matchups tab.
2. **Review**: Open your team's **Strategy Notes** in [Team Builder](/docs/team-builder) to review your gameplan against that archetype.
3. **Refine**: Re-benchmark key thresholds using [Damage Calculator](/docs/damage-calc) (using **Load from Box**), tweak EV spreads, and record a new revision.
4. **Validate**: Battle again and verify whether the Pokémon's win rate when picked trends upward in the analytics.
