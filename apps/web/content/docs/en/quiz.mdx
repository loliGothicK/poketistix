## Overview

The Quiz mode (`/quiz`) is a dedicated training hub designed to sharpen your competitive judgment, internalize complex Double Battle mechanics, and develop intuitive damage estimation.

Covering everything from basic priorities and weather-terrain interactions to high-level three-factor interactions and obscure edge cases, it offers tailored practice for every skill tier.

---

## The Three Categories

Quizzes are divided into three distinct categories based on the specific competitive skills they cultivate:

### 1. Academic

Tests your comprehensive knowledge of official battle mechanics and engine interactions:

- Ability activation orders and immunity conditions.
- Interactions between weather, terrains, and status moves.
- Redirection mechanics (Follow Me, Rage Powder, Ally Switch).
- Complex defensive type profiles and secondary effect interactions.

### 2. Damage Calc

Builds quick mental estimation of damage ranges and lethal thresholds in common competitive matchups:

- Spread damage calculations (0.75x penalty in Double Battles) and Helping Hand boosts.
- Recognizing crucial OHKO/2HKO survival lines against staple offensive threats.

### 3. Tsume (Endgame Puzzles)

A chess-like tactical puzzle format where you must choose the optimal sequence of actions to close out a game from a fixed endgame board:

- Reading Protect and switches while managing speed control.
- Calculating exact turn order and lethal damage across remaining active slots.

## The Four Difficulty Tiers

The Academic category features four structured difficulty levels tailored to your experience:

| Tier         | Target Audience                         | Example Topics                                                                                                           |
| :----------- | :-------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Basics**   | Beginners to competitive battling       | Core type charts, single-ability mechanics (Intimidate, Drought), standard status conditions.                            |
| **Advanced** | Active ladder competitors               | Essential competitive mechanics (Prankster Dark immunity, weather speed doubling, spread move interactions).             |
| **Expert**   | Tournament players & top cut contenders | Multi-variable interactions with 3+ overlapping factors (Unaware damage calculation, Foul Play stat modifiers & boosts). |
| **Master**   | Meta theorists & mechanics purists      | Hyper-obscure edge cases, niche ability interactions, and technical mechanics that rarely swing standard games.          |

## Diverse Question Formats

Rather than plain rote memorization, tests employ dynamic formats to test real-world decision-making:

- **Single Choice**: Identify the exact mechanic among tricky, realistic distractors.
- **Multi-Select**: Select all conditions or Pokémon that meet specific criteria.
- **Ordering**: Arrange Pokémon or abilities in exact operational priority or speed-tier sequences.
- **Grouping & One-Way**: High-level categorization challenges reserved for Master tier.

---

## Explanations & Mastery

Every question includes an authoritative, self-contained **Explanation** breaking down the verified game engine logic:

- Logical reasoning and official mechanics governing the outcome.
- Clarification on common misconceptions (past generation mechanics, false analogies with similar abilities).
- Strategic takeaways for live match situations.
