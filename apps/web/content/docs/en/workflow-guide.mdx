## Overview

Pokétistix is an integrated competitive ecosystem that accompanies your entire journey—from early theorycrafting and prep to post-match retrospectives and iterative team refinement.

This guide outlines the **5-step core competitive cycle** designed to build, pilot, analyze, and continually evolve your teams with zero guesswork:

> **The Competitive Iteration Cycle**
>
> **Step 1: Set Management** ➔ **Step 2: Team Building** ➔ **Step 3: Damage Verification** ➔ **Step 4: Battle Logging** ➔ **Step 5: Analytics & Iteration** ➔ (_Feedback loop to Step 1 & 2_)

| Step                              | Core Tool                                                                 | Purpose & Actions                                                                          |
| :-------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Step 1: Set Management**        | [My Box](/docs/my-box)                                                    | Stock reusable Pokémon sets and specific EV spreads in a central library                   |
| **Step 2: Team Building**         | [Team Builder](/docs/team-builder)                                        | Assemble 6-Pokémon squads, write strategy notes, and track revision history                |
| **Step 3: Damage Verification**   | [Damage Calculator](/docs/damage-calc)                                    | Load sets directly from My Box to calculate KOs, survival benchmarks, and field modifiers  |
| **Step 4: Battle Logging**        | [Battle Record](/docs/battle-record)                                      | Log matches, brings/leads, opponent teams, ratings, and tactical notes with speed          |
| **Step 5: Analytics & Iteration** | [Battle Analytics](/docs/battle-analytics) / [Dashboard](/docs/dashboard) | Discover low-win-rate matchups, analyze lead synergies, and refine your team in Step 1 & 2 |

## Step 1: Stocking Your Pokémon Sets in My Box

Before assembling teams, save your core trained Pokémon and specialized spreads in [My Box](/docs/my-box).

1. **Access My Box**: Navigate to "My Box" from the main navigation sidebar.
2. **Configure Sets**:
   - Customize Pokémon, Nature, Held Item, Ability, 4 moves, and Champions EV spreads (32 per stat cap, 66 total).
   - Alternatively, import sets in bulk from Showdown text.
3. **Build an Archetype Library**:
   - Save diverse variations of staple Pokémon (e.g., "Tailwind Support Whimsicott", "Offensive Moonblast Whimsicott"). You can later load these sets into Team Builder and Damage Calculator with a single click.

## Step 2: Assembling Teams & Writing Strategy Notes

Assemble your 6-member squad in [Team Builder](/docs/team-builder) and codify your tactical thinking in Strategy Notes.

### 1. Roster Assembly with BOX Integration

- **Load from Box**: Click the **Load from Box** button on any empty slot or header to pull your pre-configured sets into the active team instantly.
- **Fluid Search**: Use fast partial matching or direct Romaji keystrokes (such as `gabu`, `rizadon`, `sa-fugo-` when using Japanese) to find members swiftly.

### 2. Documenting Tournament Strategy Notes

Open the integrated **Strategy Notes** workspace to document key gameplans:

- **Team Concept**: The overarching win condition (e.g., speed control, Hyper Offense, bulky pivot).
- **Core Lead Pairs**: Your designated openers (e.g., "Primary Lead: Flutter Mane + Chi-Yu / Secondary: Urshifu + Ogerpon").
- **Matchup Blueprints**: Tactical adjustments for key metagame threats (e.g., "vs. Trick Room: Taunt setter turn 1 + focus down damage dealer").

### 3. Change Tracking via Revision History

- Every tweak to EVs, items, or team members creates a distinct revision snapshot.
- If an experimental adjustment fails in testing, inspect the semantic diffs and restore your previous championship-ready version with one click.

## Step 3: Verifying Benchmarks in Damage Calculator

Before taking your team into ranked ladder or tournaments, verify critical survival benchmarks in the [Damage Calculator](/docs/damage-calc).

1. **Load Trained Sets Instantly**:
   - Click **Load from Box** above either the Attacker or Defender column to inject your exact team member's stats, EVs, item, and moves.
2. **Simulate Metagame Benchmarks**:
   - Test against prevalent meta threats under authentic Double Battle conditions (0.75x spread damage reduction, Helping Hand, screens, and terrain modifiers).
   - Verify vital damage thresholds: "Does my spread survive Timid Chi-Yu's Overheat in Sun?", "Does multi-target Rock Slide guarantee a 2HKO?".
3. **Refine**:
   - If a bulk threshold fails by a fraction of a percent, adjust Effort Values and re-save your updated team revision.

## Step 4: Fast Match Logging (Battle Record)

After completing matches on ladder or in tournament play, log your outcomes immediately in [Battle Record](/docs/battle-record).

1. **Quick-Open Form**: Press the **`N`** keyboard shortcut to launch the match logger dialog.
2. **Attach Active Team**: Select your active team to display its 6 members automatically.
3. **Record Selections**:
   - Click cards to toggle **Lead (Yellow)** / **Back (Blue)** / **Benched** (or use number keys `1`–`6`).
4. **Log Opposing Team**:
   - Add opponent Pokémon to empty slots and tag their starters/reserves.
   - Click the pencil icon on opponent cards to note revealed items, moves, or critical misplays.
5. **Score & Rating**:
   - Press **`W`** (Win), **`L`** (Loss), or **`D`** (Draw), enter your post-match rating, and press **`Ctrl + S`** (`Cmd + S`) to save.
6. **Active Party Panel**:
   - The party panel at the top shows your team's live cumulative win rate badge, giving you instant feedback on team performance.

## Step 5: Data Analysis & Continuous Refinement

Once you have logged a series of matches, review your analytical data in [Battle Analytics](/docs/battle-analytics) and [Dashboard](/docs/dashboard).

### 1. Pokémon Pick Rates & Win Rate When Picked

- **Identify Dead Weight**: Members with under 15% selection frequency likely suffer from redundant roles or bad positioning in the current meta.
- **Find Your True MVP**: Spotlights Pokémon with staggering win rates when brought, helping you refine your standard lead protocols.

### 2. Opponent Matchup Breakdown

- Identify opposing Pokémon that cause your win percentage to plummet, pinpointing structural weaknesses in your composition.

### 3. Closing the Loop: Iteration

1. **Update Strategy Notes**: Revisit your team's notes in Team Builder and formulate clear counter-strategies for problematic matchups.
2. **Adjust Moves & Spreads**: Re-calculate surviving lines in the Damage Calculator and adjust EVs.
3. **Save as a New Revision**: Advance to the next version snapshot and continue testing.

Following this cyclical workflow turns raw match experience into structured competitive mastery.
