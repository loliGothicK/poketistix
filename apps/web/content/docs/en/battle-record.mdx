## Overview

The Battle Record feature helps you quickly log and manage the outcomes of your Pokémon battles on a match-by-match basis.
You can organize your records by seasons, link teams directly from the Team Builder, track lead/back selections, and record tactical notes.
See the [Practical Workflow Guide](/docs/workflow-guide) for an end-to-end tutorial.

## Creating a Season

Before you can start recording battles, you need to create a **Season**.

1. Click **"New season"** in the sidebar or top header.
2. Set the season name, format (**Singles** or **Doubles**), ruleset, and time period.
3. Click "Save" to finish creating it.

## Recording a Match

With a season selected, click the **"Record battle"** button or press the **`N`** key on desktop to open the dialog.

### Information You Can Log

| Field           | Description                                                               |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Outcome         | Win (`W`) / Loss (`L`) / Draw (`D`)                                       |
| Your Team       | Select the team you used from the [Team Builder](/docs/team-builder)      |
| Selection       | Tap your Pokémon to toggle roles (Unselected / Back: Blue / Lead: Yellow) |
| Opponent's Team | Register up to 6 opponent Pokémon and their lead/back roles               |
| Opponent Intel  | Click the pencil icon on an opponent card to record items, moves, etc.    |
| Rating          | Your post-match rating (delta is automatically calculated)                |
| Notes           | Document key plays, misplays, and match reads                             |
| Tags            | Archetype/gimmick tags (e.g., `trick-room`, `tailwind`, `weather-rain`)   |

### Recording Selections

Tap each Pokémon in your team panel to toggle its selection status:
(`Unselected` → `Back` (Blue) → `Lead` (Yellow) → `Unselected` ...)
Opponent Pokémon cards can be tapped in the same way to record their selections.

### Keyboard Shortcuts

| Hotkey                         | Action                                         |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **`N`**                        | Open new battle record dialog (from list view) |
| **`W`**                        | Select result as Win                           |
| **`L`**                        | Select result as Loss                          |
| **`D`**                        | Select result as Draw                          |
| **`1`** – **`6`**              | Toggle selection for your team members         |
| **`A`**                        | Add opponent Pokémon (search dialog)           |
| **`Alt + 1`** – **`Alt + 6`**  | Toggle opponent selection (or search if empty) |
| **`Alt + R`**                  | Reset all opponent slots                       |
| **`Ctrl + S`** / **`Cmd + S`** | Save record                                    |
| **`Esc`**                      | Close dialog                                   |

## Active Party Panel & Team Win Rate Badge

The active party panel at the top of the interface (optimized for both desktop and mobile viewports) displays your currently selected team's Pokémon icons and team label.

- **Active Team Win Rate Badge**: Displays your active team's cumulative win percentage as a dynamic color badge, letting you immediately gauge team health and momentum.
- **Instant Team Switching**: Switch between teams in the dropdown to instantly re-focus on matches played with that specific configuration.

## Match History & Analytics Navigation

- **Stats Bar**: View current rating, rating deltas, overall win rate, and the W/L/D bar breakdown at the top of the page.
- **Filters**: Quickly isolate matches by "All", "Win", "Loss", or "Draw".
- **Analytics**: Click "Analytics" in the header to jump to [Winrate Insights](/docs/battle-analytics) or custom [Dashboard](/docs/dashboard) to discover matchup trends.

## Deleting Records

You can delete a match record by clicking the delete button on its detail page. This action cannot be undone.

## Login and Cloud Sync

If you are logged in, your battle records are securely stored in the cloud.
We highly recommend creating an account if you want to access your logs from multiple devices.
