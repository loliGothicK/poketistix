【Explanation】
In Pokémon Champions, the interaction has been updated: when an Eject Button is triggered by damage from an opponent's U-turn, the Pokémon holding the Eject Button switches out first to a Pokémon in reserve, and subsequently the user of U-turn can now also switch out normally. The Eject Button no longer prevents the opponent's pivot move from switching out.
