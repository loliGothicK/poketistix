As part of building our battle simulator (`engine`) for Pokétistix, we conducted a thorough verification of the **End-of-Turn resolution order** in the Pokémon Champions format.

In Double Battles, weather damage, healing, residual damage, field expirations, and end-of-turn abilities all stack up at the end of a turn. Knowing **which effect resolves first** can directly decide whether a Pokémon survives or faints.

Here is the verified End-of-Turn timeline along with the most important takeaways for players.

---

## Core Rule: Speed Order Within Each Step

End-of-turn effects resolve in a fixed sequence of phases. Whenever multiple active Pokémon trigger effects within the same step, they resolve in **Speed order** (fastest first, or slowest first while Trick Room is active).

If a Pokémon faints partway through the end-of-turn sequence, it is skipped for all remaining steps.

## 2 Key Interactions That Decide Matches

Among all end-of-turn steps, two timing interactions stand out as genuine game-deciders in Double Battles:

### 1. When Roost and Electric Terrain Wear Off

At the end of a turn, effects resolve in this exact order:

1. **Yawn (Drowsy)** (Phase 8)
2. **Roost ends (Flying type restored)** (Phase 8, after Yawn)
3. **Terrain expires (Electric Terrain, etc.)** (Phase 9)

Because both Roost and Electric Terrain remain active until _after_ Yawn resolves, even on the **final turn of Electric Terrain**, a Flying-type Pokémon that used Roost is still grounded while Electric Terrain is still up—meaning **it is protected from falling asleep to Yawn**.

### 2. Perish Song Timing

Two ordering relationships around **Perish Song** (Phase 8) frequently decide Double Battle endgames:

- **Resolves _before_ Trick Room expires**
  - When the last remaining Pokémon on both sides hit Perish count 0 on the same turn, they faint in **Speed order** (slowest first under Trick Room), and **the last side to faint wins**.
  - Because Perish Song (Phase 8) resolves _before_ Trick Room wears off (Phase 9), on the **final turn of Trick Room**, Trick Room is still active during the Perish Song check—meaning the slower Pokémon faints first and the **faster side survives last to win**.
- **Resolves _after_ residual damage (Sandstorm, Poison, Burn, Salt Cure, etc.)**
  - All residual damage phases (Phases 1–6) tick before Perish Song (Phase 8). Even if you hold the winning Speed tie-breaker for Perish Song, fainting to residual damage beforehand immediately loses the game, making final-turn HP thresholds critical.
